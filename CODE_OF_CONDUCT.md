# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreement directly.
- Protect private data, credentials, prompts, and security-sensitive details.
- Give credit and accept responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of
private information, and sustained bad-faith interaction are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `bufeibufei@users.noreply.github.com`.
Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or ban contributions and participants
whose behavior conflicts with this policy.
